# Build Wheel design contract

Visual thesis: a quiet, centered text draw. The idea itself is the event.
Night Signal adapts the restrained Pen composition into Snack Overflow colors.

## Structure

- First view: Build Wheel identity, a category control, one large
  waiting-state title, and one clear Spin button.
- After spin: selected idea, one-sentence build premise, a grouped action row
  with the one-minute timer as the highlighted center action, share action, and
  a clear path for the next person to spin.
- Footer: the series contract; six text-only platform links live in the header.

## System

- Charcoal `#201f20`, white `#faf9ff`, lavender `#a89df6`, and purple
  `#7c6feb`. No gradient or decorative cards.
- Display and body: Inter; utility labels: IBM Plex Mono.
- No cards or navigation maze. Use alignment, type scale, and negative space
  for hierarchy.
- Motion and sound: brief text roulette with a soft tick per title, then a
  settled result cue. The post-result one-minute speech countdown has a clear
  audible alarm at zero. Sound starts only after user action and can be muted.
  Respect reduced-motion preferences and make every action keyboard-operable.

## Boundaries

- Mobile-first; the text draw and actions must remain usable at 320px wide.
- Do not borrow the old timed-speaking/deck dashboard directions.
- Every public spin must be genuinely random within the chosen category. The
  unlisted first-video take lands on a pinned topic without an extra on-screen
  mode label or public navigation.
- Do not add fake activity or popularity indicators.
